## Identity rules and evidence

- Company canonical form: NFC Unicode equivalence, whitespace collapse, casefold.
  No punctuation/suffix stripping, accent removal, transliteration, fuzzy matching,
  parent/subsidiary inference or recruiter/client aliasing.
- Retain raw name, source ID, source company ID (`company_source_id`, falling back
  to `raw.company_id`), source job ID, URL and original row fields.
- Union constraints: canonical company; prepared `posting_id`; source-scoped job ID;
  canonical posting URL using the existing tracking-only URL rules; prepared
  `repost_group_id`; explicit `duplicate_of` references to supplied `row_id`.
  Shared source company IDs also constrain groups. Repost links can connect distinct
  canonical company names without claiming those names are aliases.
- A posting/duplicate identity with contradictory company names, a source company
  ID with contradictory names, or one source/name with contradictory company IDs
  marks the entire connected group for review. Missing company/source/posting,
  unresolved duplicate links and supplied `entity_conflicts` also quarantine the
  entire group. No first-row resolution. Existing preparation review reasons are
  preserved separately as `preparation_review_reasons`; ordinary missing salary
  does not become a company identity conflict.
- Missing posting identity uses a stable evidence fingerprint independent of
  row ordinals. Identical unresolved evidence is linked conservatively and stays
  in review; its fingerprint is not an assertion of a verified posting identity.
- Group IDs depend on sorted identity keys, not row order. Locations and skills
  do not multiply observations. Summary reports raw rows, distinct entity identity
  keys (including unresolved evidence fingerprints), canonical names, connected
  groups and review groups. Source-specific posting IDs remain distinct evidence
  identities even when a shared URL links them into one indivisible group.
- Output: `rows`, `aliases`, `groups`, `links`, `summary`, version, policy and four
  matching CSV strings (`csv`, `aliases_csv`, `groups_csv`, `links_csv`). The CLI
  JSON carries the full report; `--output` writes row evidence CSV. The page shows
  all tables with four download buttons. CSV list/dict cells use JSON.

`ready_for_split` means identity constraints have no identified conflict in this
report, not that salary/readiness gates permit training. Future ML06 must assign
whole ready groups to one partition, quarantine review groups, and ensure no
company crosses train/validation/test. Target ratios remain 70/15/15, seed 42;
never cut a group to force ratios. This report performs no split, fit, model
selection or numeric support-threshold selection.
